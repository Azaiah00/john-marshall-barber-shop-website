Heritage

Nine decades and counting. One address.

Questions

Before you sit down

Straight answers to what people ask most. Anything else, just call the shop.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Appointments are recommended. If you would like to walk in, call ahead at (804) 649-7577 to check the wait before you head over.

How much is a haircut at The John Marshall Barber Shop?

The last published price for a cut is $24. A cut and shampoo is $30, a cut and shave $45, a shave $22 and a shoe shine $7. Prices can change, so call to confirm.

What are the shop’s hours?

Monday through Friday 8 AM to 8 PM, Saturday 8 AM to 5 PM, and closed Sunday. Please call ahead to confirm, especially around holidays.

Where is the shop, and where do I park?

The shop is at 502 E Franklin St, on the ground floor of the historic John Marshall building at 5th and Franklin in Downtown Richmond. Directory listings mention free lot parking; call the shop to confirm current parking before you come.

Do you offer hot-towel straight-razor shaves?

Yes. A hot-towel straight-razor shave is $22 on its own or $45 with a haircut (last published prices).

Do you cut women’s hair?

Yes, women’s cuts, including classic bobs, are offered. Call ahead to talk through what you would like.

Do you accept credit cards?

Directory listings show American Express, Visa, Mastercard and Discover as accepted. Call the shop to confirm.

How long has the barber shop been open?

Since 1929. The shop opened around the day of the stock market crash, on the ground floor of the Hotel John Marshall, which opened that October.
