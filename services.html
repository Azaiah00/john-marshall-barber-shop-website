The shave

What a hot-towel shave involves

A classic straight-razor shave generally follows the same unhurried rhythm. Tell your barber about any preferences or sensitive skin before you begin.

  1. Recline

    You settle back into the chair and the barber takes a look at your beard and skin.

  2. Hot towel

    Warm towels soften the whiskers and open up the skin for a closer, more comfortable shave.

  3. Lather

    A rich shaving lather is worked in to lift the hair and protect the skin.

  4. The razor

    A straight razor, used with a steady, practiced hand, takes it all off.

  5. The finish

    A final towel and a clean finish, and you’re ready for the rest of the day.
